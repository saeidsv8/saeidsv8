# Saeid Sadeghi Vilni

**Postdoctoral Researcher | Aalto University, Finland**

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Information and Communications Engineering at Aalto University, Finland.

My research focuses on wireless communications, real-time monitoring, and optimization of communication systems. I am particularly interested in goal-oriented communications, resource allocation, and learning-based scheduling.

## Research Interests

- Goal-oriented wireless communications
- Real-time status updating and remote tracking
- MIMO and wireless resource allocation
- Reinforcement learning and optimization
- Markov decision processes and partially observable systems
